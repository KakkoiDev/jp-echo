# Kanji families — dictionary coverage audit

Audited on **2026-10-12** against Echo's bundled JMdict-derived `words-data.js` (source snapshot dated 2021-04-17). This is a reproducible **coverage comparison**, not a claim that every written-character match shares the same etymology.

- **206 semantic parent kanji**, covering **99 distinct dictionary indexing radicals** (not all 214 traditional Kangxi radicals)
- **696 prepared family–word links**, each pointing to one of **694 original example sentences**. Some examples are intentionally shared.
- **10,315 additional family–word matches** found in Echo's existing **22,954-entry** dictionary, counted by family. One word can match several family parent characters; these are not all unique new vocabulary entries.
- **205 of 206** families have more dictionary matches than prepared examples.
- All dictionary matches can now be inspected in the Kanji family sheet, with search, reading, meaning and JLPT-level filters. **Only prepared examples have ready-made sentences.**

## Selected clear omissions now included as example sentences

**発生（はっせい）**, 発展, 発達, 発明, 発売, 発行, 爆発, 発電, 発想, 発車, 発射, 発言; **生** now also includes 学生, 生徒, 生まれる, 人生, 誕生日; **会** now includes 社会, 会話, 会員, 会場, 機会. Additional clear omissions were added across 行, 未, 動, 開, 意, 関, 技, 不, 人, 手, 出, 学.

## Scope and caution

- **Radical (部首)** is dictionary indexing metadata, not an assertion that every compound's meaning derives directly from the radical. For instance, 発's dictionary radical is 癶, while its useful vocabulary family is 発.
- The extended list consists of **attested dictionary matches**, not manually curated semantic derivations. It includes inflections, proper nouns, specialized words and several senses. JLPT level labels are inherited from Echo's word list and are not an official unified JLPT vocabulary syllabus.
- The dictionary snapshot isn't an exhaustive list of modern Japanese. Some valid words may not be included. Reading examples are drawn from dictionary entries; no synthetic reading or example sentence is supplied for additional dictionary-only entries.
- This curriculum has 206 semantic parent kanji, **not** 206 dictionary radicals. The 214 conventional Kangxi radicals are a different, indexing-oriented syllabus.
