        Band order

          When it is not on the wall

          Readings, meanings, the dictionary and the bands are on the device — looking one up works with the plane door shut. Only writing a new sentence, or asking Echo for a note, needs the network.

          MoraPronunciation anchors

          When a word refuses to stick, Echo turns its sounds into the same Japanese images every time, then makes them move in order so the action carries the meaning. The images are scaffolding: let them disappear once the Japanese comes directly.
