              RadioReview · while you sleep

              Echo plays the sentences you have studied, newest first, while you fall asleep and through the night. Plug the phone in and leave Echo open: the screen stays on, dark. Set the volume low — a cue you can barely hear is the one that helps.

              Radio plays only sentences you have reviewed at least once. Review a few first, then come back tonight.

                Why this order: replaying sentences you have studied during deep sleep, early in the night, helps memory a little; hearing the translation straight after each one cancels that help. So the meaning comes in the wind-down and again late in the night, never in the deep-sleep hours.

                MoraPronunciation anchors

                When a word refuses to stick, Echo turns its sounds into the same Japanese images every time, then makes them move in order so the action carries the meaning. The images are scaffolding: let them disappear once the Japanese comes directly.

                Tap a sound to hear it with its anchor. Test yourself hides the anchors until you tap. English draws the a · i · u · e · o headings and the word under each anchor; Furigana adds the anchor’s reading; Emoji adds a picture.

                Voiced — the same anchors, giant (゛) or as a balloon (゜)

                ゛ 巨大 きょだい giant. Picture the anchor huge and heavy: が is an umbrella so big it blots out the sky, ぐ a bear the size of a house. Voiced sounds feel big and heavy in Japanese: ゴロゴロ is a boulder rolling, コロコロ a pebble.

                ゜ 風船 ふうせん balloon. Picture the anchor blown up as a balloon that pops: ぱ is a flower balloon going pop. P is a popping sound.

                Combined sounds — one anchor each

                Most combined sounds have a picture of their own. Voiced ones reuse it as a giant, p-sounds as a balloon: じょ is a giant 醤油, びょ a giant 豹, ぴゃ a balloon 百. A sound without one fuses its two anchors.

                Small っ

                っきって = 木 → 手×2doubles the next consonant; no anchor of its own

                ゛ 濁点 = 巨大 giant, heavy゜ 半濁点 = 風船 a balloon that popsん = 頷き a nod, “un”
