Focus and motion One gesture, one meaning

Movement in this app means exactly one thing: audio is playing right now. Everywhere else the ripple is drawn and still. Nothing moves to be pretty.

The gesture — emanate

One ring leaves the centre on each pass of the loop, grows, and fades to nothing. Two forms of the same motion: full rings where there is room, an arc pair where it sits in a row of text. Same clock, same easing, same fade — so they read as one idea, not two.

Full rings
Four, because four is what the canvas draws. Cycle 2.4s · ease-out Stagger 0.6s — 2.4 ÷ 4, so the cadence is even Scale 0.24 → 1 Opacity 0.9 → 0.3 at 65% → 0 Origin: the centre. In SVG that is not the default — set transform-box: view-box and transform-origin: center on the animated element itself, or the rings fly out of the top-left corner. It is not inherited from a parent group.
Arc pair
Three copies of one arc, scaling outward — not three arcs breathing. Same 2.4s, stagger 0.8s. The arcs no longer pulse in place; that was a second gesture doing the same job and the two never agreed.

A still screen stays still. The rings behind a title or a due count are ornament — part of the drawing, like the paper texture. They do not animate, ever. If everything ripples all the time, a moving ring stops meaning anything, and the one place movement is information — the loop — loses its signal.

Why nobody saw it — and the weights that fix it The build shipped this CSS verbatim and it runs. It was invisible, not absent: the only moving echo on a playing screen was the arc pair — 76px wide, cropped to 42px, a 2px stroke at 0.234 — and the rings only ever appeared on screens where nothing plays. Two rules, both measured: Full rings (1px, 196px and up): 0.16 still, 0.234 moving — a ring spends most of its cycle fading, so moving strokes carry more. On a phone, behind a sentence, 0.30. Arc pair (1.6px, 96 × 48, never cropped below 48px): 0.5 still, 0.7 moving, and its fade ends at 0.15, not 0 — three arcs on a 0.8s stagger otherwise all vanish together once a cycle. Where: every screen where audio can play carries the rings behind the sentence and gets .is-playing — Practice, the checked review, the sentence page, Discussion, Reading. Rings on a screen where nothing plays stay ornament.

Focus ring

A 2px seal-red ring, 3px clear of the control, on :focus-visible only — outside the border, so nothing shifts when focus lands. Tab through this board to see the real thing.

On dark the ring lifts to #E0614B — same 2px, same 3px gap.

When motion is off

Nothing is lost. Under prefers-reduced-motion the arcs freeze at the three positions above — which is simply how they are drawn on every other artboard. Playback is still announced by the seal-red dot, the word Playing, and the Pause label. Motion was never the only carrier.

This also settles the platforms. Android's battery saver turns the whole OS down to reduced motion below roughly 15% battery, and some browsers drop CSS animation entirely — on those devices Echo is a still app, and a still app is a complete one. No fallback animation, no JavaScript path.

Settings · Appearance Three values: follow my device, always on, off. It exists so a phone in battery saver can still be asked for the movement back — the device setting is a good default, not a verdict.
