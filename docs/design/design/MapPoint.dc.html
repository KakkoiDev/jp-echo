N3 In a sentence 〜てしまう To do completely · to do by accident, regrettably Bunpro N3, lesson 1 · in 2 of your sentences, neither learned yet

In one breathAdjust

Attach しまう to the て-form. It says the action is finished for good — and, very often, that you wish it weren’t: 食べてしまった is “I ate it all” or “I went and ate it”, depending on the face you make. In speech it shrinks to 〜ちゃう / 〜じゃう.

A way to remember itAdjust

Shimau sounds like “shut, man!” — the door slams shut behind the action. Done. Nothing to be done about it now, which is exactly the shrug in the regret sense.

Echo’s, until you make it yours.

Two to try

  • 財布を家に忘れてしまった。 I went and left my wallet at home.
  • 本を一晩で読んでしまいました。 I finished the whole book in one night.

Keep one and it becomes a sentence of yours — scheduled, reviewed, backed up and exported to Anki like any other. Until then it is only a suggestion and lives nowhere.
