The rule

A source is anything with a licence line and a fetch rule. Echo chooses which source to ask and checks the entry against it; the chip says who answered. An entry Echo wrote itself is marked Echo, kept beside the others, and never mistaken for a dictionary.

Everything here is included in backups. Nothing leaves the device except the one lookup you asked for.
