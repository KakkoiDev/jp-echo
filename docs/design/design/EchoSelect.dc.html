The rule

Echo appears on the thing under your finger — a word, a reading, a sentence, a note, a label. If there is no thing, there is no Echo. Every verb opens the editor a person would use, with the seal beside it; nothing is sent until you press the seal.

The toolbar is the only new surface. Each verb lands in a sheet the app already has.
