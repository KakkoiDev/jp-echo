Saved words — the states

Saved is a mark, not a fourth review state. Met and learned still come only from the learner’s sentences, so a saved word can be any of them at once: the bookmark says saved, the existing chip says met or learned, and nothing says unmet. One new reusable piece, SavedMark; the state chips are the existing word-state chips.

The mark

saved · 12pxnot savedbutton, offbutton, on

SavedMark — one drawn bookmark, 12×16 viewBox, stroke 1.4. Off: --sumi outline. On: filled --seal. The button form is the IconButton with aria-pressed: on is the seal-tint ON state, like the flag. Rows show the 12px mark, never a button.

Removing

Tap the pressed bookmark, or “Remove from saved words” at the foot of the sheet. The mark goes back to its outline, one status line says “Removed from saved words.”, and the word leaves the Saved words list without a toast. A tombstone keeps it removed across devices. Saving again brings back its contexts.
