Accessibility contract, as drawn

One filled button per state. Every percentage or score names its denominator (“18 of 24”, “6 h of 7 h 10 min”). Kanji in prose carry readings or a tappable word. Play/pause sit in the bottom half for one-handed reach. The recall ring and ripple are static under reduced motion. A saved grade is announced once (role=status), a failed one once (role=alert). Focus order follows reading order; every icon button is named.
